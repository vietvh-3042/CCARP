Anthropic separates the attacks into two threat models, and the right defence depends on which one you face. In the first, jailbreaks and direct prompt injection, the user of your application is the adversary and writes inputs designed to get around your rules. Third, apply least privilege so a successful injection does little harm: don't give Claude secrets it doesn't need, run tools in sandboxes, and scope permissions narrowly.
