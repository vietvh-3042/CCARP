leading-relaxed text-foreground">Agent loops are expensive because every turn resends the entire conversation so far: system prompt, tool definitions and every prior turn. A 40-turn task sends its first turn 40 times, so cost grows with roughly the square of the number of turns. Caching doesn't stop the resending. It makes each resend cheap: the cached prefix is billed at the cache-read rate, and only new content pays the 1.25x write rate. On most models a read costs a tenth of the input price, on Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Sonnet 5.5 a twentieth, and on Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1 a fortieth. Cached prefixes are also processed faster, so the same change cuts latency too.
