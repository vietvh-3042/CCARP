terms</h2><p class="mt-4 leading-relaxed text-foreground">The two basic retrievers fail in opposite ways. <strong class="font-semibold">Embeddings</strong> turn text into vectors so that chunks close They are good at semantic relationships, but they can miss an exact match that matters.</p><p class="mt-4 leading-relaxed text-foreground"><strong class="font-semibold">BM25</strong> is a lexical ranking function: it looks for the exact words or phrases in the query. BM25 adds a correction for document length and a saturation function on term frequency, so common words cannot dominate the ranking. That makes it strong wherever queries carry unique identifiers or technical terms.</p><p class="mt-4 leading-relaxed text-foreground">Take the query "Error code TS-999" against a technical support database.
