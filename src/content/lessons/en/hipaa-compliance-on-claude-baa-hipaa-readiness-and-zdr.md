It needs a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) and a HIPAA-enabled organization. It does not work by deleting data right away. It applies a broader set of safeguards than ZDR: encryption, access controls and audit logging. An organization that processes PHI uses HIPAA readiness and does not also need ZDR.</p><p class="mt-4 leading-relaxed text-foreground">Most organizations can turn it on themselves. The controls apply as soon as you accept, and the setting is <strong class="font-semibold">permanent</strong>: an administrator cannot turn it off. Organizations that need a negotiated BAA go through the sales team instead.</p><p class="mt-4 leading-relaxed text-foreground">Once HIPAA readiness is on, the API enforces the feature list.
