output should know that AI helped produce it. Content that originates with AI is becoming common, and signals about where content came from give readers useful context for judging it. Whoever ships a Claude-based product is responsible for that disclosure. Saying the AI wrote something explains nothing and excuses nothing, because the builder owns the outcome.</p><p class="mt-4 leading-relaxed text-foreground">Disclosure works on two levels. The human-facing level is yours to design: a label in the interface, a line in a generated report, an onboarding note saying responses are AI-assisted. The machine-readable level is a mark embedded in the content itself, so software can check it after the content has left your product. Anthropic has signed the EU AI Act's Article 50(2) Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content, which commits it to machine-readable marking of Claude output.
