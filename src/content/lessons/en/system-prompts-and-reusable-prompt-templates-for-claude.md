For chatbots and knowledge bases, add a tagged block of retrieved entries (for example, a <code class="rounded bg-muted px-1 py-0.5 font-mono text-[0.9em] text-foreground [overflow-wrap:anywhere]">&lt;kb&gt;</code> section) to ground answers in a fixed information set.
