The questions about users, needs, experience and constraints are covered in <a class="text-primary underline underline-offset-2 hover:no-underline" href="/en/lessons/writing-measurable-requirements-and-acceptance-tests-for-ai-solutions/">The discovery brief: users, needs, experience, constraints</a>. Otherwise the numbers measured during design say nothing about production.</p><p class="mt-4 leading-relaxed text-foreground">An eval suite written in discovery also settles disagreements early.
